# Risk Brief — individual presentation (15%)

> **Four minutes. One development. One view. One firm. One recommendation.**

Each student delivers one Risk Brief, at the start of one of Sessions 02–11. Sign-up opens at the end
of Session 01; four briefs per session.

---

## The brief

You are the geopolitical risk analyst of a named multinational. Something happened in the last
**four weeks**. Brief your chief risk officer.

| Minute | What you say |
|---|---|
| **0:00–0:30** | **The development.** What happened, where, who decided it. One sentence of context, no history lecture. |
| **0:30–2:00** | **The view.** One Tableau chart, built from data **you collected at source**, that puts the development in perspective — is it large, new, accelerating, concentrated? |
| **2:00–3:00** | **The exposure.** Through which channel does it reach *your* firm — market access, inputs, finance, assets and people, data and operations? How much of the firm sits in the path? |
| **3:00–4:00** | **The recommendation.** One decision, and the indicator and threshold that would make you change it. |

One question follows (one minute), from the room or the instructor. Four briefs fill the first
twenty minutes of the session.

**The firm.** Any real multinational with public information on its footprint (annual report,
10-K, sustainability report). It may — but need not — be your team's firm.

**The data.** Any source in [`DATA-SOURCES.md`](../../DATA-SOURCES.md) or any other public source,
cited with version and access date. Ideally the concept or dataset of a session you have already had.

---

## What you hand in

Before class on the day: the `.twbx` (or a Tableau Public link) and your provenance log, on
ZoneCours.

---

## Rubric (15 points)

| Criterion | Points | Full marks look like |
|---|---|---|
| **The development** | 2 | Precisely dated, sourced, and genuinely geopolitical (between states or political actors) |
| **The view** | 4 | Data collected at source; correct aggregation; title states the finding; source caption; the chart *does* work in the argument |
| **The exposure** | 4 | A specific channel; evidence from the firm's own disclosures; an order of magnitude |
| **The recommendation** | 3 | One decision, an indicator and a threshold that would reverse it |
| **Time and question** | 2 | Four minutes, not six; answers the question asked |

**Automatic deductions:** no source caption (−2); over five minutes (stopped at five); a development
older than four weeks (−2).
