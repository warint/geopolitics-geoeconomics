# Take-home examination (40%)

> **Three hours of work, within a 48-hour window, during the final examination period. Individual.
> Open book, open notes, open internet. No collaboration of any kind.**

---

## The format

At the opening of the window you receive, on ZoneCours:

- a **scenario** — a geopolitical development, real or plausible, dated;
- a **firm** — a real multinational, with an extract from its disclosures;
- a **data instruction** — one or two sources from [`DATA-SOURCES.md`](../../DATA-SOURCES.md) to
  collect from, with the variables to use.

You hand in, before the window closes:

| Part | Points | What |
|---|---|---|
| **A · Concepts in context** | 12 | Three short essays (≈ 300 words each) applying the course's concepts to the scenario: e.g. what realism and liberal institutionalism each predict; whether sanctions in this case are likely to work; how the firm's standards exposure changes |
| **B · The evidence** | 14 | One packaged Tableau workbook (`.twbx`) built from data you collected, with its provenance log, and a 400-word reading of it |
| **C · The assessment** | 14 | A 900-word memo to the firm's chief risk officer: the exposure, the interactions between risks, the decision, the watchlist with thresholds |

**Length limits are strict.** Words beyond the limit are not read.

---

## Marking criteria

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| Correct and precise use of the course's concepts | 30% |
| Data collected at source, correctly prepared, honestly logged | 25% |
| Analysis of interactions between risks and their implications for the firm | 25% |
| A decision with triggers, defensible from the evidence | 20% |

---

## On language models and integrity

You may use a language model to check syntax or to help you read a codebook. You may not use it to
produce any text you submit. Every submission includes a one-paragraph statement of the tools you
used and how. Undeclared use, or collaboration with another person, is a violation of HEC Montréal's
academic integrity regulations.

## Specimen scenario

*Dated 15 March. The European Union and the United States announce coordinated export controls on
advanced battery-manufacturing equipment to a major Asian economy; that economy responds within a
week with an export licensing regime on refined graphite.*

**Firm:** a European automotive manufacturer with an EV assembly plant in Central Europe and a joint
venture in the affected Asian economy.

**Data instruction:** OECD Inventory of Export Restrictions on Industrial Raw Materials (graphite,
HS 2504 and 3801); UN General Assembly ideal points (Bailey, Strezhnev & Voeten); the firm's last
annual report.

*Part A (example):* Using Farrell and Newman's concept of weaponised interdependence, explain which
side holds the stronger *chokepoint* and why. *Part B:* Build a view of the concentration of
graphite exports and of the export restrictions applied to it since 2009. *Part C:* Advise the chief
risk officer on the joint venture and on sourcing for the European plant.
