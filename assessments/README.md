# How the course is graded, on one page

| Component | Weight | Individual? | Form | When | Brief |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Participation** | 10% | yes | active engagement, continuous | every session | below |
| **Quizzes** | 15% | yes | 3 × 5%, 20 min, in class, on paper, closed book | Sessions 04, 07, 10 | [`quizzes/`](quizzes/README.md) |
| **Risk Brief** | 15% | yes | 4-minute presentation + one question (a 5-minute slot) | one of Sessions 02–11 | [`risk-brief/`](risk-brief/README.md) |
| **Team work** | 20% | no | risk dashboard for one multinational + memo + presentation | presented Session 12 · memo one week later | [`team-project/`](team-project/README.md) |
| **Take-home examination** | 40% | yes | 3 hours of work within a 48-hour window | final examination period | [`take-home/`](take-home/README.md) |

**80% individual · 20% team.** In HEC Montréal's terms: A07 Participation (10%), A03 Tests and
quizzes (15%), A06 Individual presentation (15%), A09 Team work (20%), A11 Take-home examination
(40%).

## Participation — 10%

Marked against a published, absolute bar — never ranked:

| Mark | What it looks like across the term |
|---|---|
| **9–10** | Regularly moves the discussion forward with reference to the reading; asks real questions after Risk Briefs; fully engaged in every practice round |
| **7–8** | Contributes often; engaged in practice rounds; occasional questions |
| **5–6** | Present and engaged when asked; rarely volunteers |
| **< 5** | Disengaged, or absent from practice rounds without reason |

Practice rounds are **formative**: commented on, not marked. What is recorded is that you engaged
and handed in the `.twbx` and provenance log.

---

## Late work and missed assessments

- **Quiz** missed with a documented reason: its weight moves to the take-home. Without: zero.
- **Risk Brief** missed with a documented reason: rescheduled. Without: zero.
- **Team memo**: −5% of its mark per day late.
- **Take-home**: submissions after the window closes are not accepted, per HEC regulations.
