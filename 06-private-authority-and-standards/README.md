# Session 06 — Private authority, standards and global regulation

### Who writes the rules a multinational actually follows?

> **Most of the rules that govern a multinational's daily operations were not written by a
> government. Who wrote them, with whose power — and does trade spread them up or down?**

## What session 06 is for

| Geopolitics | Data & Tableau | International business |
|---|---|---|
| Place a standard-setting scheme in **Abbott and Snidal's governance triangle** — states, firms, NGOs | Download (or transcribe) the ISO Survey and normalise counts by GDP | Treat certification as a **condition of market access**, set by buyers and their home markets |
| Explain, with **Mattli and Büthe**, why international standards reflect the institutions — and power — of those who write them | Map adoption by ISO code; fix the countries Tableau cannot place; animate with **Pages** | Read the competition over technical standards (telecoms, batteries, AI) as **geopolitical** risk |
| Distinguish **delegated** from **entrepreneurial** private authority (Green) and the three perspectives on standardization (Brunsson et al.) | Build an **export-weighted** measure of export-market adoption with a double join | Decide when to adopt, when to shape, and when to resist a private standard |
