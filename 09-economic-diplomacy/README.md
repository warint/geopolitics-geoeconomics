# Session 09 — The redefinition of economic diplomacy

### Two-level games, digital diplomacy, and embassies as export promotion

> **When states compete for markets, investment and technology, is the multinational the object of
> diplomacy, its instrument — or a diplomat in its own right?**

## What session 09 is for

| Geopolitics | Data & Tableau | International business |
|---|---|---|
| Explain Putnam's **two-level game** and the **win-set** | Turn a post-level file into a dyad-level count | Map the instruments of **commercial diplomacy** a firm can use: posts, trade missions, export credit, investment promotion |
| Explain how **digital diplomacy** changes Level II, after Bjola & Manor | Join posts to trade on two country fields, keeping the zeros | Explain **investment screening** as economic diplomacy aimed *at* firms |
| Situate economic diplomacy between states, and between **states and firms** (Stopford & Strange) | Read a log-scale scatter and a trend model honestly — including **reverse causality** | Decide when a firm should lean on its home state — and when that is itself a risk |
