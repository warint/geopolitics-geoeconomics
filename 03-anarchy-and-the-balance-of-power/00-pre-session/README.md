# Session 03 — Pre-session preparation

> **In a system with no government above states, who sets the terms of trade — and how would a
> multinational know that the balance is shifting?**

Slides: [`INTE60019A-S03-Pre-Session.qmd`](INTE60019A-S03-Pre-Session.qmd)

Three things to do before class. Budget **about two hours**.

| | Before class | Used in class for |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Read Mearsheimer and Allison | the lecture discussion and the replication |
| **2** | Collect NMC v7.0 and SIPRI, read the NMC codebook | Practice round 03 |

---

## 1. The reading — 60–75 min

**Mearsheimer, J. J. (2001). *The Tragedy of Great Power Politics*. W. W. Norton.** Chapter 1
(*Introduction*) and Chapter 2 (*Anarchy and the Struggle for Power*). The five bedrock assumptions
are in Chapter 2: copy them.

**Allison, G. (2017). *Destined for War: Can America and China Escape Thucydides's Trap?* Houghton
Mifflin Harcourt.** The Introduction and Chapter 2 (*Athens vs. Sparta*), and the appendix listing
the sixteen cases.

Annotate four things:

| | What to look for |
|---|---|
| **1** | Mearsheimer's **five assumptions** — and which one does the most work |
| **2** | How each author **measures power**: what counts, what is left out |
| **3** | Allison's **sixteen cases** — how many ended in war, and how a case was chosen |
| **4** | One claim a **multinational with operations in both the US and China** should take seriously, and one it should doubt |

**The target figure** is the distribution of capabilities among the major powers since 1816 — the
picture that underlies both books' argument that the balance is shifting.

---

## 2. The data — 20 min

Download and unzip **NMC v7.0**, and download the latest **SIPRI** military expenditure workbook.
Details and terminal commands: [`../data/README.md`](../data/README.md).

**Read the NMC codebook before opening the data.** Find: the six components and their units; how
CINC is computed; the last year covered. Then find the codes for China, Russia/USSR, Germany and the
UK in the COW state list (a separate file — see the data README).

---

## 3. Self-check — 15 min

Answer on paper.

1. "Anarchy" in realist theory means what? What does it *not* mean?
2. Waltz and Mearsheimer are both realists. On what question about how much power states want do
   they disagree?
3. CINC averages six shares. Name the three dimensions they cover. Which dimension do you expect to
   flatter a large, populous, industrialising country?
4. Allison counts how many cases of a rising power challenging a ruling one, and how many ended in
   war? Write the numbers down — then write one reason to doubt the ratio.
