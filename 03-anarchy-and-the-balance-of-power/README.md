# Session 03 — Anarchy and the balance of the major powers

### The geostrategic balance that dictates international trade relations

> **In a system with no government above states, who sets the terms of trade — and how would a
> multinational know that the balance is shifting?**

## What session 03 is for

| Geopolitics | Data & Tableau | International business |
|---|---|---|
| State what **anarchy** means in realist theory — and what it does not | Unzip and read a codebook before opening the data | Say why the balance of power sets the conditions of market access, sanctions and export controls a firm faces |
| Distinguish **defensive** (Waltz) from **offensive** (Mearsheimer) realism, and both from **power transition** theory | Build a share-of-system measure and a power ratio with calculated fields | Read a power transition as a change in **which rules your firm will be asked to follow** |
| Assess Allison's **Thucydides's Trap** and its critics | Use a **parameter** so the board can choose the challenger | Explain why three measures of power date the US–China transition three different years — and which one matters for a firm |
