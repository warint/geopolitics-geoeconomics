# Session 03 — Teaching plan (first half, 90 min)

# Anarchy and the balance of the major powers

> **Instructor page.** The student-facing companion is [`README.md`](README.md); the deck is
> [`INTE60019A-S03-Lecture.qmd`](INTE60019A-S03-Lecture.qmd).

*In a system with no government above states, who sets the terms of trade — and how would a
multinational know that the balance is shifting?*

---

## Opening — Risk Briefs (20 min)

Four briefs, five-minute slots (four minutes + one question). After the last one, ask: *"Was any of these four
about the distribution of power between states?"* — it usually is, and it bridges into the lecture.

---

## Board plan

| Minutes | |
|---|---|
| **20–30** | **Anarchy.** Write the word, then ask what it means. Correct "chaos" immediately. Self-help, uncertainty about intentions, relative gains. Land relative gains on export controls on chips. |
| **30–45** | **Two realisms.** Ask who copied Mearsheimer's five assumptions; have one student read them. Which does the most work? (Uncertainty about intentions, usually.) Draw the defensive/offensive table. The security dilemma as a two-by-two drawn by hand. |
| **45–60** | **Power transition and Allison.** The 80% threshold on the board. Sixteen cases, twelve wars — then put the critics to the room before showing them: *"What is wrong with this ratio?"* Selection on the outcome, nuclear weapons, interdependence, measurement. |
| **60–75** | **CINC.** Six components, shares, average. Ask: *which two components flatter China?* Then predict out loud: in which decade does CINC show China passing the US? Do not answer — the practice round does. |
| **75–85** | **What it means for the firm.** The five implications. Spend time on "two compliance regimes": what does a firm do when complying with one jurisdiction's rules is a violation of the other's? |
| **85–90** | **What the figure does not license**, then open Tableau: create the `Challenger` **parameter** live and show it on the view. Hand over. |

---

## Run Tableau live

Create the parameter from the `stateabb` field (*Create → Parameter*, list of values, *Add values
from* `stateabb`), then a calculated field that uses it. Show the parameter control changing the
view. Show the trap too: a COW code that looks like ISO but is not (`GMY`).

---

## Put this to the room

*"You are the strategy director of a European industrial firm with 30% of revenue in China. A board
member says 'China has already overtaken America.' What do you ask before you answer?"*

Push for: overtaken **on what** measure, **when**, and **which of our decisions** depends on it.

*For the instructor only (NMC v7.0, checked October 2026):* China's CINC first exceeds the US's in
**1995**; in 2022 it is 0.234 against 0.124. Let the practice round find it.

---

## Misconception to pre-empt

That realism predicts war. It predicts **competition**, and — in Waltz's version — often restraint.
Say it after the two-realisms table, and again when Allison's ratio appears.
