# Session 04 — Trade, conflict and geoeconomic fragmentation

### Does trade keep the peace — and is the world now trading along geopolitical lines?

> **If states trade more with friends than with rivals, how does a multinational find out which side
> each of its markets is on — and how fast the lines are hardening?**

## What session 04 is for

| Geopolitics | Data & Tableau | International business |
|---|---|---|
| Set the **liberal commercial peace** against its **realist critique**, and state what Mansfield's review concludes | Relate a dyadic table (exporter × partner × year) to a country table **twice** | Classify a firm's markets by geopolitical alignment, and say what friend-shoring implies for each |
| Explain Martin, Mayer & Thoenig's distinction between **bilateral** and **multilateral** trade openness | Define blocs with **sets** whose thresholds are **parameters** | Distinguish "de-coupling" from "de-risking" and say which the data show |
| Describe how Gopinath et al. **define blocs** and measure fragmentation | Index a series to a base year with a table calculation | Identify **connector** countries and the opportunities and risks they carry |
