# Session 10 — Economic sanctions

### Do they work — and what do they do to the firms caught between sender and target?

> **Sanctions have become the instrument of first resort. Are they an efficient mechanism for
> regulating geopolitical risk — or a source of it?**

## What session 10 is for

| Geopolitics | Data & Tableau | International business |
|---|---|---|
| Classify sanctions by **sender, type, objective** and **target** (comprehensive vs targeted) | Turn episodes (start, end) into **active cases per year** with a scaffold | Explain the firm's compliance perimeter: lists, ownership rules, **secondary sanctions** |
| State Hufbauer et al.'s **success** finding and Pape's **critique** | Pivot type dummies; count distinct cases; stacked bars and **percent of total** | Explain **overcompliance** and de-risking — why firms go further than the law |
| Explain the **selection effect** of threats (Drezner) | Draw one target's history as a **Gantt chart** | Frame an **exit decision**: Russia 2022 |
