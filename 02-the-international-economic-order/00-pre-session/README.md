# Session 02 — Pre-session preparation

> **Who wrote the rules multinationals play by — and what happens to the rules when the writers'
> share of the world shrinks?**

## 1. The reading — 60–75 min

**Baldwin, R. (2016). *The Great Convergence: Information Technology and the New Globalization*.
Belknap Press of Harvard University Press.**

Read the **Introduction** and **Part I** (the long history of globalisation in four phases) and
**Part II** (the new globalisation and the "three cascading constraints"). Skim Part IV on policy.
The book is on reserve at the HEC library; the e-book is available through the library catalogue.

Annotate four things:

| | What to look for |
|---|---|
| **1** | Baldwin's **three costs** of moving goods, ideas and people — and which one fell in each unbundling |
| **2** | Why the **first unbundling** produced divergence and the **second** convergence |
| **3** | The **figure** showing the G7 share of world income — what is on the axis, what is measured |
| **4** | One claim a **G7 multinational's strategy team** would contest, and why |

**The target figure** is the G7 share of world GDP. Note its units (current US dollars? constant?
PPP?), its start and end years, and its source. You will need all four.

---

## 2. The data — 15 min

Build a custom extract in World Bank DataBank: the G7, the "Industrialising Six", World; three
series; 1970 to the latest year. Step-by-step: [`../data/README.md`](../data/README.md).

Open it once and find: the year the G7's GDP first appears for every member, how missing values
are written, and the last year with a value for *World*.

---

## 3. Your team's firm — 15 min

Teams of three or four form in the first ten minutes of the practice round. Arrive with a
**shortlist of three multinationals** you would want to follow all term. A good candidate:

- publishes an annual report or 10-K with **revenue by country or region**;
- has operations, suppliers or customers in **at least three of** the US, the EU, China, Russia,
  the Gulf, India;
- is exposed to at least one of the course's channels: sanctions, export controls, cyber, standards.

No two teams may adopt the same firm. Registration on ZoneCours is first come, first served.

---

## 4. Self-check — 15 min

Answer on paper.

1. What did the Bretton Woods conference of 1944 create, and what did the GATT add in 1947?
2. In one sentence each: what was unbundled in Baldwin's first unbundling, and what in the second?
3. GDP shares can be measured at market exchange rates or at purchasing-power parity. Which will
   show the G7 with a **larger** share, and why?
4. Rodrik's trilemma says you can have at most two of three things. Name the three.
