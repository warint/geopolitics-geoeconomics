# Session 01 — Teaching plan (first half, 90 min)

# What is geopolitical risk?

> **Instructor page.** The student-facing companion is [`README.md`](README.md); the deck is
> [`INTE60019A-S01-Lecture.qmd`](INTE60019A-S01-Lecture.qmd).

*Before we can manage geopolitical risk, what exactly are we counting?*

---

## Opening

Before any slide, ask the room: *"Name one geopolitical event of the last five years that cost a
company money. Which company, and through which channel?"* Write three answers on the board —
they will reappear in section 5 as examples of the five channels.

---

## Board plan

| Minutes | |
|---|---|
| **0–15** | **The syllabus.** Big theme, rhythm, grade. Show the twelve-session table and the arc. Insist on two things: the licence request, and the rule that every practice documents one disagreement between the data and the paper. |
| **15–30** | **Risk, uncertainty, three kinds of risk.** Knight on the board. Then the three-row table. Place the opening examples in it: is Russia 2022 political, country or geopolitical risk for a Canadian bank? (All three — make them say why.) Kobrin's macro/micro, then the third layer: risk from *outside* the host country. |
| **30–45** | **Six families of indicators.** Build the table live; ask for one example per family before revealing it. End on *triangulation*: the team project must use at least three families. |
| **45–65** | **The GPR index.** The definition, word for word — ask who copied it. The four steps of construction. Ask: *why divide by the total number of articles?* (Newspaper size changes; the share does not.) Threats vs acts. Validation by narrative and by hand audit. Country indices. |
| **65–78** | **Why a firm should care.** Real options in two minutes: irreversible investment + uncertainty → value of waiting. The five channels table, linked to the sessions where each returns. |
| **78–90** | **What GPR does not tell you.** Four limits. Make the room add a fifth. Then open Tableau on the projector and do the first two steps of the practice live: connect, check the date type. Hand over. |

---

## Run Tableau live

Connect to the file in front of them and let Tableau guess the types. In some releases `month`
arrives as a string or a date-time: fix it on screen, and say that this is exactly the kind of thing
the provenance log is for.

---

## Put this to the room

*"The GPR index for March 2022 was several times its long-run average. If you ran a Canadian
fertiliser exporter, what would you actually do differently that month?"*

Do not accept "monitor the situation". Push for a decision — hedge, delay, reroute, diversify — and
for the threshold that would trigger it.

---

## Misconception to pre-empt

That a high GPR reading means *risk is high for my firm*. It means the anglophone press is writing a
great deal about geopolitical tension. Say it at the start of section 4 and again at the end.
