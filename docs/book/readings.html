Appendix D — Readings

The required reading of each session is its big work: the article or book whose central figure the practice round rebuilds. It is read and annotated before class. Further readings are for the Risk Brief, the team memo and the take-home; none is required.

Article–data map: REPLICATIONS.md. Datasets: DATA-SOURCES.md.
