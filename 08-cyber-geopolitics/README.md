# Session 08 — Cyber geopolitics and digital threats

### Restraint, rivals and weaponised networks

> **Is cyberspace a new battlefield for great powers — or a new way for old rivals to hurt each
> other, with multinationals as collateral?**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Big work** | Valeriano, B., & Maness, R. C. (2015). *Cyber War versus Cyber Realities: Cyber Conflict in the International System*. Oxford University Press. [doi:10.1093/acprof:oso/9780190204792.001.0001](https://doi.org/10.1093/acprof:oso/9780190204792.001.0001) |
| **Companion** | Farrell, H., & Newman, A. L. (2019). Weaponized interdependence: How global economic networks shape state coercion. *International Security*, 44(1), 42–79. [doi:10.1162/isec_a_00351](https://doi.org/10.1162/isec_a_00351) |
| **Target figure** | Incidents by year, method and severity — and the **initiator × target matrix** of rival dyads |
| **Data you collect** | Dyadic Cyber Incident and Campaign Data (DCID) v2.0, [doi:10.7910/DVN/CQOMYV](https://doi.org/10.7910/DVN/CQOMYV); EuRepoC Global Dataset v1.3, [doi:10.5281/zenodo.14965395](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.14965395) |
| **Tableau skill** | Highlight tables · dyad matrices (initiator × target) · filter and highlight actions |
| **Course themes** | The evolution of cyber geopolitics and digital threats in the decision-making architecture of multinational firms |

---

## What session 08 is for

| Geopolitics | Data & Tableau | International business |
|---|---|---|
| State the **cyber-restraint** argument of Valeriano & Maness and the evidence behind it | Read an event dataset's codebook: unit of observation, dyad, method, severity | Place cyber risk in the firm's decision architecture: board, CISO, insurer, regulator |
| Explain the **panopticon** and **chokepoint** effects of weaponised interdependence | Build a highlight table and an initiator × target matrix | Explain why firms are hit by operations not aimed at them (collateral damage) |
| Explain why **attribution** makes cyber events a peculiar kind of evidence | Link two sheets with a filter action | Assess what data localisation and digital-sovereignty rules mean for a multinational's architecture |
